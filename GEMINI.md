# Project & Workspace Instructions

## Typography & Punctuation Rules
- **Never use the em-dash (`—`, `&mdash;`, or `\u2014`)**:
  - Do not use the em-dash anywhere in this codebase or in any project communications.
  - This applies to copy, titles, meta tags, labels, buttons, notifications, error messages, alt text, comments, documentation, and assistant responses.
  - Acceptable alternatives:
    - Standard hyphen (`-`) or spaced hyphen (` - `)
    - Colon (`:`)
    - Semicolon (`;`)
    - Comma (`,`)
    - Parentheses (`(...)`)
    - Separate sentences
